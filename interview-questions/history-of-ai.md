## The mathematical roots, 1763 to 1913

Machine learning's oldest ideas come from probability and astronomy: Bayes' theorem, published in 1763 after his death, Legendre's least squares in 1805, Laplace's 1812 treatise on probability, and Markov's chains, first applied to real data in 1913.

### Was Bayes's Theorem published after his death?

Yes. Bayes died in 1761, and his friend Richard Price edited his essay and presented it to the Royal Society in December 1763, two years later. Pierre-Simon Laplace, working independently, stated the same principle in 1774 and developed it further over the following decades, notably in his Théorie analytique des probabilités (1812).

[Read the longer answer →](https://www.datasciencetrivia.com/questions/was-bayess-theorem-published-after-his-death)

### The least squares method was first published by which famous French mathematician?

Adrien-Marie Legendre.

[Read the longer answer →](https://www.datasciencetrivia.com/questions/the-least-squares-method-was-published-by-a-famous-french-mathematician-called)

### In 1812 Laplace published "Théorie analytique des probabilités", in which he stated a famous theorem. Which theorem was it?

Bayes' theorem, which Laplace had found independently in 1774 and set out here as a general principle for inferring causes from observed events. The same book is also known for Laplace's central limit theorem.

[Read the longer answer →](https://www.datasciencetrivia.com/questions/in-1812-laplace-published-theorie-analytique-des-probabilites-in-which-he-defines-a)

### Andrey Markov first described the techniques he used to analyse a poem, and these techniques later became known as Markov chains. True or false?

False as stated. Markov introduced the chains in a 1906 paper on the law of large numbers for dependent variables. His 1913 study of vowel and consonant sequences in the first 20,000 letters of Pushkin's Eugene Onegin was their first application to real data, not their origin.

## Early AI and the first neural networks, 1950 to 1989

From Turing's test in 1950 and the first neural network machine a year later, through the perceptron and the reverse-mode differentiation behind backpropagation in 1970, to LeCun's convolutional networks and Watkins's Q-learning, both from 1989.

### When was the Turing Test created?

1950, in Turing's paper "Computing Machinery and Intelligence", which swapped the question of whether machines think for an imitation game judged over text. Its weakness is that it rewards convincing conversation rather than understanding — a critique now aimed straight at LLMs.

[Read the longer answer →](https://www.datasciencetrivia.com/questions/when-was-the-turing-test-created)

### When was the nearest neighbor algorithm created?

1951: Evelyn Fix and Joseph Hodges introduced nearest neighbor classification in a US Air Force report. The often-quoted 1967 is Thomas Cover and Peter Hart's paper proving that 1-NN's error is at most twice the Bayes error.

[Read the longer answer →](https://www.datasciencetrivia.com/questions/when-was-the-nearest-neighbor-algorithm-was-created)

### In 1951 Marvin Minsky and Dean Edmonds conceived what is known as the first neural network machine. What is its name?

### Who invented the perceptron?

Frank Rosenblatt. Bonus. The invention of the perceptron generated a great deal of excitement and was widely covered in the media.

[Read the longer answer →](https://www.datasciencetrivia.com/questions/who-invented-the-perceptron)

### What did Seppo Linnainmaa's 1970 master's thesis introduce?

> **Flashcard:** In 1970 Seppo Linnainmaa described reverse-mode automatic differentiation in his master's thesis. This corresponds to the modern version of:

Backpropagation — reverse-mode automatic differentiation, which gets the gradient of a scalar loss with respect to every parameter in roughly one backward pass. Rumelhart, Hinton and Williams rediscovered and popularised it for neural networks in 1986.

[Read the longer answer →](https://www.datasciencetrivia.com/questions/in-1970-seppo-linnainmaa-published-the-general-method-for-automatic-differentiation-this)

### Who is known as the founding father of convolutional nets?

Yann LeCun, who at AT&T Bell Labs in 1989 first trained convolutional networks end to end with backpropagation (later LeNet). The architecture builds on Kunihiko Fukushima's 1980 neocognitron.

[Read the longer answer →](https://www.datasciencetrivia.com/questions/who-is-known-of-the-founding-father-of-convolutional-nets)

### When was Torch released?

2002, when Ronan Collobert, Samy Bengio and Johnny Mariéthoz at IDIAP described the C++ library. The better-known Lua-based Torch7 followed in 2011.

### Who coined the term deep learning in 2006?

Geoffrey Hinton is usually credited: his 2006 deep belief net paper with Osindero and Teh made the term popular. Strictly, he didn't coin it. Rina Dechter used "deep learning" in 1986, and Igor Aizenberg and colleagues applied it to neural networks in 2000.
